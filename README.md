# Midlands Table: Business Analysis

Business analysis for a free public website where people can find South African recipes and plan their meals.

**Author:** Nkosinathi Emmanuel Ndlovu
**Date:** November 2026
**Status:** Complete, ready for build

> The restaurant, the people and the findings in this project are fictional. They were created for this business analysis project.

## About the project

Ukudla Kitchen is a small restaurant in Pietermaritzburg that serves home-style KwaZulu-Natal food. Customers often ask the owner for her recipes, but she has no easy way to share them. She wants a website for the public, not for the restaurant, where anyone can find these recipes and use them.

This repository holds the business analysis for that website, called **Midlands Table**.

## Decision

Lab 1 compared three options and recommended building a small recipe website. The requirements in Lab 2 describe version 1 of that website.
